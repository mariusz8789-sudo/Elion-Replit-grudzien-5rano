## Remaining genuine blockers and product gaps

1. **Premium anatomy asset:** a legally usable, segmentable model with separately identified skin, muscles, skeleton, vessels, nerves and organs is required to approach the supplied Human Explorer reference. No purchase was made.
2. **Environment asset quality:** professional architecture, ruins, people, scientific props, terrain and planet materials are needed to move the historical, alien, Mars and city worlds beyond procedural prototypes.
3. **Physical Mirror validation:** no real camera/face-tracking validation exists on the target user hardware.
4. **Optional scientific runtimes:** PySCF is not installed for Python 3.14 on this Windows host. Optional docking/ADMET/OpenMM/Vina/Meeko/Biopython/DepMap paths depend on their configured runtimes/data and remain fail-closed when absent.
5. **Film finishing:** subtitle and narration cues are metadata, not burned-in subtitles or a rendered voice track. The eight new prompt worlds have browser screenshots; they are not yet directly routed into their own MP4 jobs.
6. **TAA reset:** current renderer reports `temporalAccumulation: NOT_PRESENT`. There is no accumulation history to reset, but the strict inherited capability remains BLOCKED rather than being scored as a pass.
7. **Scientific fidelity:** procedural anatomy/micro geometry and spacetime visuals communicate structure and model state; they do not certify medical precision, microscopy resolution, real dark-matter observation or time travel.
